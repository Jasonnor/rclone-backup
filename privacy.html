Privacy policy: rclone personal backup

Last updated 16 November 2026.

Who uses the app

Only the owner of the Google account that created it, for their own backup.

What it can access

Only the Google Drive files the app itself created (Google's drive.file permission). It cannot see any other file in the Drive, nor the account's email, contacts or other data.

How the data is used

rclone uploads the owner's files into the owner's own Google Drive, encrypted on the owner's computer before they are sent, lists them to keep the copy current, and downloads them when the owner restores them.

Where it is kept

The files stay in the owner's Google Drive. The sign-in token stays on the owner's computer.

Sharing

No data from Google is sent to anyone other than Google, sold, used for advertising, or used to train any model.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
